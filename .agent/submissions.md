Long: Routine Meeting is a free macOS menu-bar app that detects meetings by the mic plus system-audio pattern of a real conversation, so it works with Meet, Zoom, Teams, Webex, Slack huddles, Discord and FaceTime with no per-app setup. Recording and transcription (Parakeet v3, optional WhisperKit) run entirely on the Mac. Optional summaries use your own Gemini key (text only) or a local model. No account.
