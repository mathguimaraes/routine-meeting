Routine Meeting 1.11 replaces the old home screen with one built from all your meetings together, not one call at a time.

**The new home page**

It gives you the big picture of what you are discussing, organized into broad areas of work such as product, bugs and performance, customers, sales, marketing and operations. Each area has a dated timeline, the decisions made and the questions still open, and it updates after every call.

It runs on your Mac by default using Apple's built-in on-device model, so nothing leaves the machine. A cloud key is optional.

**Removed**

Task Radar, Insights, the morning "yesterday recap" notification and the Daily Summary menu item are gone. The new home page replaces them.

**Fixes**

- On-device summaries no longer show raw `<li>` tags.
- Better cleanup of Whisper repetition loops and `[BLANK_AUDIO]` tags in transcripts.
- Gemini requests retry on the next model after a 400 error.
